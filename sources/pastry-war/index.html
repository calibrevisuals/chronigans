The PETTIEST Reason France Ever Invaded a Country

The Pastry War, Mexico, 1838-1839

Everything we say in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • —

    Mexico-France correspondence, 1837-1839, published by INEHRM in "La Guerra de los Pasteles"Foreign minister Cuevas's refusal to pay (1837), the Paris newspaper line about a trade treaty (1838) and the Mexican negotiators' report on the peace deal (1839), with INEHRM's day-by-day chronology.

  • —

    Carlos María de Bustamante's account of the leg's funeral (1842)The column, the urn, the little cannon, the eagle and the key to the urn, from a historian who was there. Quoted in Garza Becerra (2010).

  • —

    Fanny Calderón de la Barca, Life in Mexico (1843)An eyewitness to Santa Anna after the battle and to his false leg in 1841. Her footnote quotes a Havana newspaper on the leg's burial on 27 September 1842.
