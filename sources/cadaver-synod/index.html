Legends and disputed points

  • 2:19

    Formosus's oath never to return to Rome (Troyes, 878). The record is shaky and comes from his enemies' side.

  • 4:40

    That Stephen cancelled Formosus's acts to save his own position. A modern theory; no source from the time says so.

  • 4:56

    Pressure from Lambert and Agiltrude versus personal hatred. Historians still disagree on Stephen's motive.

  • 6:03

    The judge's exact words. Only Liutprand reports them, about 60 years later.

  • 7:19

    The earthquake hitting mid-trial. The quake is recorded during Stephen's time; 'mid-trial' is a later embellishment.

  • 7:32

    Who pulled the body from the Tiber: a monk or a fisherman, depending on the source.

  • 8:28

    Saints' images bowing to the body. Liutprand's story only.

  • 9:32

    Sergius ordering Leo V and Christopher strangled. Reported by a pro-Formosus writer; likely, not proven.

  • 10:11

    Sergius fathering a future pope. A rumour, said as one.

  • 10:57

    Pietro Barbo wanting to be Formosus II. One cardinal's story.

Some retellings say the corpse's head fell off during the trial. We found no source for that, so it isn't in the video.
