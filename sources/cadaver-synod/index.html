The Dumbest Trial a Pope Ever Did in History (897 AD)

The Cadaver Synod, Rome, 897

Everything we say in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • —

    Liutprand of Cremona, Antapodosis I.30 (written about 960)The judge's question to the corpse, and the story of the bowing saints. Written about 60 years later by a writer who was not there.

  • —

    Synods of Pope John IX at Rome and Ravenna (898)The decrees that cleared Formosus, burned the trial records and banned trials of the dead, as summarised in the Catholic Encyclopedia.
