Some retellings say Midas was Greek, that the daughter is in the ancient myth, or that archaeologists found Midas's own tomb full of gold. None of that holds up: he was Phrygian, Hawthorne added the daughter in 1851, and the big tomb at Gordion held no gold and most likely belonged to his father, Gordias.
