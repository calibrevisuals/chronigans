Sailing in The Odyssey SUCKS actually... (Animated)

The Odyssey from the rowing bench: every stop of Odysseus' trip home, and how the crew died, as Homer's poem tells it

Everything we say in this episode comes from Homer's poem, read in the text itself. The movie comparisons come from the reviews listed below.

The Odyssey is a myth, not history, and the video treats it that way. It never claims a crew total (Homer gives none), never says Odysseus ate the cattle or allowed it (he was asleep), never says the crew killed them for no reason (they had starved for a month and offered a temple), and never says for certain that nobody told them what was in the bag: Homer doesn't say either way.
